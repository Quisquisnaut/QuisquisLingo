# Lesson icon provenance

The PNG files in this directory were created specifically for QuisquisLingo
with OpenAI ImageGen through Codex on 2026-09-02. No third-party source artwork
was supplied or incorporated.

The QuisquisLingo project grants permission to use, copy, modify, and
redistribute these fourteen generated icon files, commercially or non-commercially,
with or without the application and without an attribution requirement. They
are provided without warranty.

Each production icon is a 256 × 256 px, transparent, non-interlaced RGBA PNG.
The generated source was normalized to a four-color solid palette for the
flat, no-gradient Lesson-icon standard.

## The sixteen icons of Build 264 (series 2)

`animals.png`, `nature_weather.png`, `body_health.png`, `clothing.png`,
`sports.png`, `music.png`, `art_culture.png`, `colours_shapes.png`,
`numbers_maths.png`, `emotions.png`, `city.png`, `world_countries.png`,
`technology.png`, `celebrations.png`, `alphabet_writing.png` and
`questions_conversation.png` were drawn for QuisquisLingo by the project
owner with Claude (Anthropic) in October 2026, from QQL's own
specification, in the same style as the fourteen icons above (palette
`#301040`, `#9030F0`, `#10B0A8`, `#F0C040`, with cream where needed). No
third-party source artwork was supplied or incorporated, and the same
permission applies to them.

They keep their smooth, anti-aliased edges (owner decision of 6 October
2026), so the Lesson-icon standard checked by `tools/validate_lesson_icons.py`
is a flat style: at most twelve main colour groups covering at least 90% of
the opaque pixels, rather than four exact colours.
