# Media credits

Version 0.7.3

The in-app **Credits** page keeps the primary olive image directly visible and links to a separate **Image credits** page for other visual assets.

## Main olive background

Olea europaea plate from *The North American Sylva* (1819), François André Michaux et al. Wikimedia Commons. Public domain / Public Domain Mark.

## Other images

The in-app Image credits page lists the public-domain olive artwork, decorative lesson plants, the built-in Flat Image Bank, programmatically drawn Status avatars, and three flat multicolor Lesson icons generated specifically for QuisquisLingo with OpenAI ImageGen on 2 September 2026. The generated Lesson icons use no third-party source artwork; their repository-local provenance is recorded in `assets/lesson_icons/LICENSE.md`. See `PUBLIC_DOMAIN_IMAGES.md` for the olive source details.

## Sounds

Duel result tones are original synthesized audio created specifically for QuisquisLingo. They are not third-party recordings.

## Course authors

Credits reserves a separate author area for each bundled course. Names are intentionally left as `To be added` until course authorship information is supplied.
